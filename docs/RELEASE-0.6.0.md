# Libertum 0.6.0

## Features

- **A story checklist.** The Notes pane has a Story tab for the beats a story has to hit. Add a point with its text, then right-click it to edit the text, add other words for it, or delete it. Each point can belong to the whole story, just this chapter, or a chosen set of chapters and everything after them; ticking it marks it done in that chapter only. As the chapter is written, Libertum scores how far each point has been covered from the words in it, counts mentions, and sorts the list by least covered or most relevant. A linked character lends their name as an extra keyword without changing the point. Scoring waits until typing has been quiet for a moment and only recounts paragraphs that changed, so a long chapter stays cheap. This is word matching, not understanding: a point can be reported as uncovered even when the idea is there in other words.
- **Characters and their plot points.** A second Notes tab keeps the cast: name, age as free text, details, and the plot points linked to each character in a pop-up. Names use AO3's `Full Name (Fandom)` form, so they can be copied straight into a tag field. A linked character's short name becomes an extra keyword for that point, and unlinking or deleting the character takes it away. The story's fandoms are kept with the cast. Search narrows the list; when there are more than fifty characters, the pane asks for a narrower search.
- **Character import.** **Import** in the Characters tab asks which fandoms the story is in, then offers a one-time, at-your-own-risk choice to look names up online. Nothing is sent until that is agreed and **Fetch** is pressed. AO3 is tried first: its work list for the fandom, newest first, up to three pages of twenty works, keeping the first fifty that have only that fandom and counting their characters, relationships and additional tags. Any failure, including AO3's bot limits, falls through to the wiki list. Wikis are read through the MediaWiki Action API after their `robots.txt` is checked, one request at a time with a pause, a bot User-Agent, a stop on slow-down or refusal, and a **Stop** button. Fandom comes first, then any sites added by the writer (https only), then Miraheze, wiki.gg, ShoutWiki and Wikipedia. Page titles become AO3 names: `Granger, Hermione` becomes `Hermione Granger`, a `(character)` qualifier is dropped and another qualifier becomes the fandom. Lists, subpages and disambiguation pages are left out, and a story with one fandom never shows names from crossovers. There is also an offline route: type or paste names, or open a small text or CSV file. The picked names, the fandoms and AO3's relationship and additional tags are saved with the story; the saved cast and tags then take part in chapter tag suggestions, ahead of the guesses made from the text alone.
- **Music players in the document.** **Insert > Music** puts a player on its own full-width line at the caret, with a title, an https link to the audio file and an optional credit. The player shows the title, elapsed and total time, a play or pause button and a bar that can be clicked to seek; it stops when the document closes. The link is what AO3 and HTML export use: `<audio controls src="…"></audio>` in the chapter fragment, a link in Markdown, and a picture in Libertum, Word, OpenDocument and RTF. A copy of the audio can be kept inside a `.lbtd` for offline use, but only after ticking a permission box and pressing **Fetch and keep a copy**: https only, `robots.txt` checked, size and time capped, and at most 30 MB. Pressing play without a kept copy fetches one for the session only. Decoding covers MP3, Ogg Vorbis, WAV, FLAC and M4A/AAC. On Linux the sound device comes from the system's PulseAudio client or ALSA, loaded at run time, so building needs no audio headers; the device is open only while a clip plays.
- **A lighter load on the battery.** Pagination and paragraph shaping now run at background priority and page rasterisation one step above it (Linux: nice 10 and 3; macOS: background and utility QoS; Windows: below-normal and EcoQoS). Pagination also waits when the reader is moving and it is already sixty pages ahead, while a jump to a far page still runs at full speed. The render queue drops pages the view has passed and draws the page ahead of travel first. An autosave tick within two seconds of the last keystroke is put off once, and leaving the window still saves at once. The 500-page demo paginated in 843 ms in a release build here, and a 6,000 px/s autoscroll held 114 to 120 frames a second with a mean frame time of 8.4 ms.

## Changes

- **Character lookup setting.** Once online lookup has been agreed to, Settings offers **Character lookup: AO3 first / Wikis only**. It is the same choice as the AO3 tick-box in the Import pop-up, so the two cannot disagree. **Wikis only** means AO3 is never contacted. Before consent, and after **No thanks**, the setting is not shown.
- **The native format keeps the new work.** A `.lbtd` can carry `checklist.json`, `characters.json` and `audio.json`, plus one stored entry per audio copy under `audio/`. The three parts are written only when there is something in them, and a damaged part is ignored on read. Other formats keep the three lists in sidecars under `.libertum-history/<file name>/`, with links but no audio copies. `view.json` now also saves the caret position.
- **New crates.** `libertum-wiki` holds the robots parser, site rules, name conversion and the whole lookup, with no HTTP client of its own so the flow can be tested without a network; requests use the system `curl`. `libertum-audio` holds decoding and playback, using `symphonia` 0.5.5 (MPL-2.0). That licence is not on the list in `CONTRIBUTING.md`; the maintainer approved it on 2026-11-26, and `docs/third-party-licences.md` records why. Its text still needs to ship with the installer's notices.
- **AO3 chapter windows.** The chapter word count leaves out the chapter heading and generated contents entries. Chapter HTML now writes the alignment chosen in the document and emits a player for music as well as a link for video.

## Packaging

- Windows: `Libertum-Setup.exe` and `Libertum-Setup-0.6.0.exe`.
- Linux: `.deb`, `.rpm` and `.AppImage`.
- macOS: `.dmg` and `.zip` for Apple Silicon and Intel.
- `SHA256SUMS` covers every attached file.
- Existing installations are offered 0.6.0 by the in-app updater; on Linux the `.deb` and `.rpm` install themselves through the desktop's administrator prompt.

## Notes

- Builds are unsigned. Windows SmartScreen may warn on first run; on macOS, right-click the app and choose **Open**.
- There is no sound output backend on Windows or macOS yet: a player there shows that playback is not available on the system. The real PulseAudio path was only tried with silence, audible playback and a fetch against a real server were not run, and an MP3 without a length header plays but has no total and cannot be seeked. A player cannot be edited in place: delete it and insert it again.
- That AO3's sanitiser still accepts `<audio>`, and which hosts it allows, is unverified. Test one chapter before relying on it.
- AO3's work-list markup was written from its known structure and has only been tried on made-up pages: the two real requests made while checking `robots.txt` both came back with the bot-limit page, so the wiki list was used. Re-check the first time AO3 answers. No browser User-Agent is faked and no more requests were made.
- Wikipedia's `robots.txt` disallows its API path for every agent, so Wikipedia is skipped and reported. Fandom answers a program with a Cloudflare challenge on `robots.txt`, which is read as no restrictions, and its API answered normally. Category names differ by wiki, so the writer may need to supply them. Fandom pages in other languages and wikis whose API is not at the usual paths are not handled.
- No energy figure was measured for the battery pass, and the Windows and macOS priority calls were not run on those systems. Nothing in it depends on whether the machine is on power or battery.
- Sidecars are named after the file, so renaming a document outside Libertum leaves its checklist, characters and music links behind. A sidecar write failure is logged rather than shown.
- Only these opt-in lookups, the daily update check and an MCP client the writer turns on use the network. Nothing about a document is sent by itself.
- No document format changed incompatibly; files opened and saved by 0.5.2 still open.
